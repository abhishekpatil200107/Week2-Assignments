About NovaEdge

A product studio of designers and engineers who love turning ideas into dependable software.

Our story

Started in a small flat, grown by word of mouth

NovaEdge began in 2018 with three friends and one belief: good software should feel simple. We started by building websites for local businesses and kept growing as those clients came back with bigger problems.

Today we’re a team of 24 working with startups and established companies across India and abroad. We still work the same way: listen first, build in small steps, and show our work often.

  1. 2018
    Founded in Pune with three people.
  2. 2020
    Launched our cloud & DevOps practice.
  3. 2023
    Reached 100 delivered projects.
  4. 2026
    Team of 24 serving clients in 6 countries.
